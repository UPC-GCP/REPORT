\section*{Conclusions and Future Work}

These document has covered the development and evolution of a Heat and Mass Transfer solver across different benchmarks of increasing complexity. Across the different stages of development of this model, different complexities regarding the implementation of numerical methods and the simplification of models have been observed and analyzed. These behaviour is consistent with the mathematical formulation behind the numerical solver and must be considered when configuring the simulation parameters. In addition, the model developed during this project will serve as a stepping stone towards more complex implementations. There are still several improvements that can be made to the model proposed with this project, particularly in terms of optimization of variables and some redundant algorithms. The model will continue to be maintained to expand its capacity and increase its performance, addressing all of the mentioned inefficiencies.
